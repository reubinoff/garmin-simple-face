# Store listing text (draft - review before submitting)

**Name:** Noir Time

**Short description (one line):**
A pure-black watch face with the date, the time, your steps and Body Battery. Pick the time size and the colours of the hours and minutes.

**Description:**
Noir Time is a minimal watch face for the fēnix® 8 and fēnix® 9 series (every size, AMOLED and Solar) and for Forerunner® watches.

• Pure black background - made for AMOLED, and clean on the Solar (MIP) screens too
• Shows the date, the time, today's step count and your Body Battery, with small icons - hide steps or Body Battery if you want
• Choose the colour of the hours and of the minutes separately - 16 colours: white, silver, red, coral, orange, gold, yellow, lime, green, teal, cyan, light blue, blue, purple, magenta, pink
• Five time sizes: Small, Medium, Large, X-Large, XX-Large - the layout always fits the round screen
• Change the settings in Garmin Connect on your phone or directly on the watch (Watch Face menu)
• Always-on mode keeps the same size with outline-only digits, and moves slightly every minute to protect the screen
• Follows your 12/24-hour setting and your watch language
• Only one permission (Sensor History, to read your Body Battery level on the watch); no phone connection needed, no ads, sends no data anywhere

**Compatibility:**
* fēnix® 8: 43mm, 47mm, 51mm, Pro (47mm, 51mm, MicroLED), Solar 47mm, Solar 51mm; fēnix® E
* fēnix® 9: 43mm, 47mm, 51mm, Pro 43mm, Pro 47mm, Pro 51mm, Pro Solar 47mm, Pro Solar 51mm
* Forerunner®: 55, 70, 165, 165 Music, 170, 170 Music, 245, 245 Music, 255, 255 Music, 255S, 255S Music, 265, 265S, 570 (42mm, 47mm), 645, 645 Music, 745, 935, 945, 945 LTE, 955, 965, 970
* Body Battery is not shown on the Forerunner 645, 645 Music and 935 (their software has no Body Battery data for apps). On the Forerunner 645 and 935 the settings are changed in Garmin Connect only.

**Permissions:** The Sensor History permission is used only to display Body Battery. No other sensor data is read, and nothing ever leaves the watch.
