# Store listing text (draft - review before submitting)

**Name:** Noir Time

**Short description (one line):**
A pure-black watch face with big Bebas Neue digits, the date and up to three data fields. Pick the time size, the colours of the hours and minutes, and the fields.

**Description:**
Noir Time is a minimal watch face for the fēnix® 8 and fēnix® 9 series (every size, AMOLED and Solar) and for Forerunner® watches.

• Pure black background - made for AMOLED, and clean on the Solar (MIP) screens too
• Big, tall Bebas Neue digits built into the face - the same look on every watch
• Three field slots with small icons - choose from steps, Body Battery, heart rate, battery %, battery days, calories, floors, intensity minutes, notifications, stress, temperature and distance, or leave a slot empty (default: steps and Body Battery)
• Choose the colour of the hours and of the minutes separately - 16 colours: white, silver, red, coral, orange, gold, yellow, lime, green, teal, cyan, light blue, blue, purple, magenta, pink
• Five time sizes: Small, Medium, Large, X-Large, XX-Large - the layout always fits the round screen
• Change the settings in Garmin Connect on your phone or directly on the watch (Watch Face menu)
• Always-on mode keeps the same size with outline-only digits, and moves slightly every minute to protect the screen
• Follows your 12/24-hour setting and your watch language
• Only one permission (Sensor History, to read your Body Battery and stress level on the watch); no phone connection needed, no ads, sends no data anywhere

**Compatibility:**
* fēnix® 8: 43mm, 47mm, 51mm, Pro (47mm, 51mm, MicroLED), Solar 47mm, Solar 51mm; fēnix® E
* fēnix® 9: 43mm, 47mm, 51mm, Pro 43mm, Pro 47mm, Pro 51mm, Pro Solar 47mm, Pro Solar 51mm
* Forerunner®: 55, 70, 165, 165 Music, 170, 170 Music, 245, 245 Music, 255, 255 Music, 255S, 255S Music, 265, 265S, 570 (42mm, 47mm), 645, 645 Music, 745, 935, 945, 945 LTE, 955, 965, 970
* Not every field exists on every watch; a field the watch cannot provide is hidden. Body Battery, stress and temperature are not available on the Forerunner 645, 645 Music and 935; floors not on the Forerunner 55, 70, 245 and 245 Music; battery days not on the Forerunner 55, 245, 245 Music, 645, 645 Music, 745, 935, 945 and 945 LTE. On the Forerunner 645 and 935 the settings are changed in Garmin Connect only.

**Permissions:** The Sensor History permission is used only to display Body Battery and stress. No other sensor history is read, and nothing ever leaves the watch.
