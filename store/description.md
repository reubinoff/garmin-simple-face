# Store listing text (draft - review before submitting)

**Name:** Clean Air

**Description:**
**Clean Air — a fresh, simple watch face. Clean and crisp like the air in the morning.**

Clean Air keeps it minimal and striking: a deep black background with bold, easy-to-read time set in Bebas Neue (SIL Open Font License). Pick separate colors for the hours and the minutes to match your style.

**Features**
- Big, crisp time in five sizes, with separate hour and minute colors (16 colors)
- Date at a glance
- Steps and Body Battery with clear icons
- Three configurable data slots (left, right, bottom) — choose from steps, Body Battery, heart rate, battery %, battery days, calories, floors, intensity minutes, notifications, stress, temperature, distance, or leave empty
- Designed for AMOLED always-on: under 5% of pixels lit, verified in the Connect IQ simulator
- Battery-conscious by design — no background work, timers, or network calls

Works across fēnix 8 and fēnix 9 variants plus a wide range of round Forerunner models.

Fresh. Simple. Clean as morning air.

**Compatibility:**
* fēnix® 8: 43mm, 47mm, 51mm, Pro (47mm, 51mm, MicroLED), Solar 47mm, Solar 51mm; fēnix® E
* fēnix® 9: 43mm, 47mm, 51mm, Pro 43mm, Pro 47mm, Pro 51mm, Pro Solar 47mm, Pro Solar 51mm
* Forerunner®: 55, 70, 165, 165 Music, 170, 170 Music, 245, 245 Music, 255, 255 Music, 255S, 255S Music, 265, 265S, 570 (42mm, 47mm), 645, 645 Music, 745, 935, 945, 945 LTE, 955, 965, 970
* Not every field exists on every watch; a field the watch cannot provide is hidden. Body Battery, stress and temperature are not available on the Forerunner 645, 645 Music and 935; floors not on the Forerunner 55, 70, 245 and 245 Music; battery days not on the Forerunner 55, 245, 245 Music, 645, 645 Music, 745, 935, 945 and 945 LTE. On the Forerunner 645, 645 Music and 935 the settings are changed in Garmin Connect only.

**Permissions:** The Sensor History permission is used only to display Body Battery and stress. No other sensor history is read, and nothing ever leaves the watch.
