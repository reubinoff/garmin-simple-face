**Description:**
Noir Time is a minimal watch face for the fēnix® 9 Pro 47mm.

• Pure black background - made for AMOLED
• Shows only the date, the time and today's step count
• Choose the colour of the time in the Connect IQ settings in Garmin Connect (white, cyan, green, yellow, orange, red, pink, purple)
• Always-on mode is dimmed and moves slightly every minute to protect the screen
• Follows your 12/24-hour setting and your watch language
• No permissions, no phone connection needed, no ads, collects no data

**Compatibility:** fēnix® 9 Pro 47mm only.
