# Clean Air

**A clean, black, AMOLED-friendly watch face for the Garmin fēnix® 8 and fēnix® 9 series and Forerunner® watches.**

Clean Air puts the time front and centre in big, tall **Bebas Neue** digits on a pure black background, with the date
above and up to three data fields below. Pick the size, the colours and the fields right on the watch or in the Garmin
Connect app.

## Features

- **Date** and **time**, with **separate colours for hours and minutes**
- **Its own typeface**: Bebas Neue digits embedded in the app (SIL Open Font License), sized for every screen, so the face looks the same on every watch; the date and fields use the watch's Roboto Condensed
- **Three field slots** (left, right, bottom), each with a small icon: steps, Body Battery, heart rate, battery %, battery days, calories, floors, intensity minutes, notifications, stress, temperature, distance - or nothing. Default: steps and Body Battery. Fields a watch does not support are hidden. The Sensor History permission is used only to display Body Battery and stress; nothing ever leaves the watch.
- **5 time sizes**: Small, Medium, Large, X-Large, XX-Large
- **16-colour palette**
- **Pure black background**, easy on the eyes and the battery
- **Always-on mode**: outline digits at the same size, with burn-in protection
- **Settings on the watch** (watch face menu) or in the Garmin Connect app, in English and Hebrew
- **Runs on 38 watches**: every fēnix® 8 and fēnix® 9 model (AMOLED and Solar) and the round Forerunner® models from the 645 / 935 on; the layout scales to each screen (208 to 466 px). See [the device list](store/description.md) and [screenshots per device](docs/screenshots/devices/)

## Build

You need the [Connect IQ SDK](https://developer.garmin.com/connect-iq/sdk/) (9.3.0 or newer) and a developer key.

```sh
scripts/keygen.sh     # once: creates your developer key
scripts/build.sh      # -> build/CleanAir.prg (fenix9pro47mm; CIQ_DEVICE=<id> for another) and build/CleanAir.iq (all watches)
```

## Install

Connect the watch over USB, copy `build/CleanAir.prg` to the watch's `GARMIN/APPS` folder, eject, then pick
**Clean Air** from the watch faces list.
